    How to use

    Tasks

    • Add — Type in quick-add and press Enter, or click "Details" for the full form.
    • Complete — Check the checkbox.
    • Edit — Click ✏ to edit all fields.
    • Delete — Click ✕ (undo available via toast).
    • Reorder — Drag tasks when sorted by Custom.
    • Subtasks — Click ☑ on a task to open its checklist, then add and tick items. Click any subtask's text to rename it in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels). Completing the task closes the whole checklist. Pomodoros stay on the parent task.

    Tags & Filter

    Add tags in the task form. Click a tag or project pill to filter — the selected pill gets a ✓ and a ring, and a "Clear filter" button appears below the list.

    Focus Score

    The share of every task you've ever created that is now finished: completed ÷ (completed + still open) × 100. Ticking a task off raises it; adding a new one lowers it until you finish it. Tasks only — pomodoros and subtasks don't count.

    Calendar

    Give a task one or more time blocks (in the task form, or by clicking an empty slot on the Calendar tab) and it appears in Month, Week and Day views. Today's blocks also show at the top of the Dashboard, where the chevron collapses the panel out of the way.

    • Repeat — a block can repeat daily, every weekday, weekly, monthly or annually, every n of those, and optionally stop on a date. Repeating blocks are marked ↻.
    • Changing one, or all — open a repeating block and pick "Just this occurrence" or "The entire series". The ✕ on the grid always skips just that one day, so you can't wipe a series by accident.
    • Two kinds of repeat — this is the calendar's, and it repeats a time slot. The Repeat field in the task form is separate: it recreates the whole task with a new due date when you complete it.

    Due Dates & Repeat

    Set a due date and optional repeat frequency. Recurring tasks auto-recreate on completion.

    Golden Task

    Click ⭐ to mark a task as golden — it pins to the top with a special glow.

    Themes

    Toggle dark/light mode with the theme button.

    Pomodoro Timer

    25 min focus / 5 min break cycles. Click the Pin button for Picture-in-Picture mode.

    Keyboard Shortcuts

    n — Focus quick-add · Esc — Close modals
